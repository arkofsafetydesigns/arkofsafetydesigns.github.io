Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the ARK project planner.

What is ark project planner?
Ark Project Planner is a project manager app to plan, create and manage project tasks and to provide a visual overview of your progress
Is the app free to use?
Yes. The app is free to use with a limit of 3 projects. Optional premium upgrades are available for unlimited projects and additional features.
Can I export my projects?
Yes! Select your project, select the Mindmap and then select the Export option
Can I add items from any Project Task?
Yes! Ark Project Planner works with any online retailer. You can add items from Amazon, Etsy, local boutiques, or any website.
How do I share my projects?
You can share your projects via a direct link, email, text message, or social media. Just tap the share button on your app.
What Platforms is Ark Project Planner is available on?
Ark Project Planner is available on Android devices
